# Photography & Imagery Art Direction

Photography is a brand asset, not decoration. In the reference library it does specific jobs: the emotional **hero reveal** (Ecovine's mark over an aerial field), **mockup/moodboard grounds**, the bento board's **lifestyle tile**, and the guidelines' **Imagery / art-direction** pages (section 6.0). Choose and treat every photo to one consistent direction. Source via Unsplash / Magnific stock — see `image-generation.md`.

## Pick one art-direction and hold it

The whole system reads as intentional only if every image shares a look. Decide up front, from the brand personality:

| Direction | Feel | Light | Palette | Subjects | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Light & fresh** | Optimistic, natural, clean | Bright, soft daylight, airy | Greens/whites, true colour | Produce, nature, open interiors, real people | Ecovine |
| **Dark & moody** | Premium, editorial, design-led | Low-key, directional, dramatic | Desaturated, near-mono + one accent | Person in dark clothing, close products, texture | Dusk mockups |
| **Clinical & calm** | Trustworthy, precise, quiet | Even, high-key, minimal shadow | Cool neutrals, one brand hue | Clean surfaces, stone, glass, tidy detail | Dentcor |
| **Playful & bright** | Energetic, human, youthful | Punchy, saturated | Bold complementary duo | Kids, motion, candid joy, props | Kidly |
| **Warm & appetite** | Inviting, sensory, hospitable | Warm, golden, close | Reds/creams/mustard | Food, hands, tables, steam | Burger storefront |

## Selection checklist

Reject a photo unless it passes all of these:
- **On-mood** — matches the chosen direction's light, palette, and subject.
- **Composition space** — has clean negative space where the logo/type will sit (for heroes and covers).
- **Authentic, not stocky** — real moments over posed clichés (this is where Unsplash beats generic stock).
- **Colour-compatible** — its dominant colours live near the brand palette, or can be graded there.
- **Resolution** — large enough for the placement (hero/print needs full-res; upscale via Magnific if needed).
- **Rights** — free/commercial licence; record attribution (Unsplash requires crediting the photographer).

## Treatment (make disparate photos feel like one brand)

- **Grade** toward the palette: nudge white balance and add a subtle tint of the anchor or accent. A light **duotone** (anchor + white) is a strong unifier for section imagery.
- **Consistent crop logic** and aspect ratios across a set.
- **Logo over image:** knock the mark out in white or a brand tint; add a soft gradient **scrim** (e.g. anchor at 0→55% opacity) if the photo is busy, so the mark stays legible. Keep clearspace.
- **Restraint:** one focal subject; avoid cluttered backgrounds that fight the mark.

## Where imagery goes

- **Hero / cover reveal** — full-bleed photo, mark centred in the negative space, ©/™ for polish (`presentation-formats.md` #3).
- **Bento board lifestyle tile** — one photographic tile among the flat brand tiles for warmth (`brand-board.html`, the `.photo` tile — swap its background for a real image).
- **Mockup grounds** — storefronts, environments, hands holding collateral.
- **Guidelines 6.0 — Imagery** — a do/don't board: 3–4 on-brand shots vs. off-brand counter-examples, plus the logo-over-image and scrim rules.
- **Moodboard (step 1)** — 6–9 photos that set the direction before any asset is made; get sign-off on the *feeling* early.

## Sourcing keywords (seed the search)

Translate the direction into concrete Unsplash/stock queries, e.g.:
- Light & fresh: `aerial farmland`, `fresh vegetables daylight`, `minimal bright interior`, `natural hands harvest`.
- Dark & moody: `dark studio portrait`, `black clothing minimal`, `low key product`, `moody concrete texture`.
- Clinical & calm: `white marble surface`, `clean modern clinic`, `soft neutral minimal`, `glass reflection light`.
- Warm & appetite: `burger close up`, `golden hour food`, `hands sharing meal`, `cozy cafe table`.

Search a few queries, pull 6–9 candidates, keep only those that pass the checklist, and grade them to one look.
