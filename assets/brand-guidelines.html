Construction & clearspace
Nova

Grid. The mark is built on a modular grid; stroke weight and counter ratios stay constant across the family.

Clearspace. Keep a margin of 1× (the cap-height of the wordmark) clear on all sides. Never crowd the mark.

Minimum size. 24 mm / 120 px for the full lockup; 16 px for the submark.
